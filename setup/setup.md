# Setup

## Desktop App

1. Go to the [download page](https://waarpbuild.vercel.app/download/)
2. Download the installer for your system:
   - **Windows:** `.exe`
   - **macOS:** `.dmg`
3. Run the installer and follow the prompts.
4. Launch WaarpBuild from your applications.

## Web App

You can also use WaarpBuild in your browser at:
https://waarpbuild.vercel.app
